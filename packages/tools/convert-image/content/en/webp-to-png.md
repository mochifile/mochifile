Add your WebP images here and download them as PNG. PNG opens in every program and keeps
transparent areas.

## How it works

1. Check that **PNG** is selected.
2. Add your WebP images. You can add up to 20 at once.
3. Download each PNG, or all of them as a ZIP file.

## Your photos stay private

Everything happens in your browser. Your images are never uploaded, and hidden details are
removed from every result.

## Tips

- **Logos and stickers** keep their transparent background as PNG. JPG would turn it white.
- **PNG files are bigger** than WebP. If the size matters and there is no transparency,
  choose JPG instead.
- The image keeps its full size in pixels.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does the PNG keep the transparent background?

Yes. Transparent areas stay transparent.

### Why is the PNG bigger than the WebP?

WebP is a more efficient format. PNG saves every pixel exactly, which takes more space.

### Is anything lost in the conversion?

No. The PNG shows exactly what the WebP showed. Nothing is added or lost in the conversion.
