Many forms, apps and older programs refuse WebP images, a format common on websites. Add your
WebP images here and download them as JPG, which is accepted almost everywhere.

## How it works

1. Check that **JPG** is selected.
2. Add your WebP images. You can add up to 20 at once.
3. Download each JPG, or all of them as a ZIP file.

## Your photos stay private

Everything happens in your browser. Your images are never uploaded, and hidden details are
removed from every result.

## Tips

- **Saved an image from a website and it came out as WebP?** This is why. Convert it here
  and it opens anywhere.
- **Transparent areas** are filled with white in a JPG. If you need them, choose **PNG**
  instead (see the "WebP to PNG" page).
- The image keeps its full size in pixels.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is a WebP image?

WebP is an image format created by Google for lighter websites. Browsers show it perfectly,
but many programs and upload forms do not accept it.

### Will the image look worse?

Hardly. The JPG is saved at a high quality. It can be a little bigger than the WebP, which
is a more efficient format.

### What happens to transparent backgrounds?

JPG cannot be transparent, so those areas become white. You will see a note when this happens
and a button to keep the transparency as PNG instead.
