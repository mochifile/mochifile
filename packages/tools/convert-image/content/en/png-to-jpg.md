PNG files from screenshots and designs can be very large. Convert them to JPG to get a much
smaller file that is accepted by almost every form and email.

## How it works

1. Check that **JPG** is selected.
2. Add your PNG images. You can add up to 20 at once.
3. Download each JPG, or all of them as a ZIP file.

## Your photos stay private

Everything happens in your browser. Your images are never uploaded, and hidden details are
removed from every result.

## Tips

- **Photos and screenshots** become much smaller as JPG. Drawings with flat colours and sharp
  text can look slightly softer.
- **Transparent areas** are filled with white, because JPG cannot be transparent. A note tells
  you when this happens.
- The image keeps its full size in pixels. To fit a size limit, use "Compress an image to a
  target size" afterwards.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why is the JPG smaller than the PNG?

PNG keeps every detail exactly, which makes big files for photos. JPG keeps what the eye
notices and drops the rest, so the file is much smaller.

### Will the text in my screenshot stay readable?

Yes, at the quality used here. If you need perfectly sharp text, keep the PNG.

### What happens to a transparent background?

It becomes white. You will see a note and a button to keep the transparency as PNG instead.
