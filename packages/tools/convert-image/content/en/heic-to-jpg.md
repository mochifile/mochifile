iPhones save photos as HEIC, a format that many Windows PCs, Android phones and websites
cannot open. Add your HEIC photos here and download them as JPG, which opens everywhere.

## How it works

1. Check that **JPG** is selected.
2. Add your HEIC photos. You can add up to 20 at once.
3. Download each JPG, or all of them as a ZIP file.

Your photo keeps its full size in pixels and comes out the right way up. The first photo can
take a little longer on devices that cannot read HEIC themselves, because the page loads a
small reader first, once.

## Your photos stay private

Your photos never leave your device; nothing is uploaded. Hidden details such as the place
where each photo was taken, which iPhones store in every picture, are removed from the JPG.

## Tips

- **Photos from the Photos app on Windows or Android:** if the file ends in `.heic` or
  `.heif`, it works here.
- **Sharing a photo with someone who cannot open it?** Send them the JPG.
- **Transparent HEIC images** become JPG with a white background. Choose **PNG** to keep the
  transparency.
- **Prefer your iPhone to save JPG from now on?** See the questions below.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is a HEIC file?

HEIC (also written HEIF) is the photo format Apple uses by default since iOS 11. It keeps
the same quality as a JPG in a file about half the size, which saves space on your phone.

### Why can't I open it on my computer or phone?

HEIC is not supported everywhere. Many Windows computers, Android phones, older programs and
websites do not open it or refuse it in upload forms. JPG works on all of them.

### How do I make my iPhone save JPG instead?

Open **Settings**, then **Camera**, then **Formats**, and choose **Most Compatible**. New
photos will be saved as JPG. Photos you already took stay HEIC, so you can convert them here.

### Will I lose quality?

No visible loss. The JPG is saved at a high quality and with the full size in pixels. The file
may be bigger than the HEIC, since JPG is a less efficient format.

### Does this work with Live Photos or videos?

It converts the still photo only. Videos are not supported on this page.
