WebP files are lighter than JPG, which helps websites and online shops load faster. Add your
JPG photos here and download them as WebP.

## How it works

1. Check that **WebP** is selected.
2. Add your JPG photos. You can add up to 20 at once.
3. Download each WebP, or all of them as a ZIP file.

## Your photos stay private

Everything happens in your browser. Your photos are never uploaded, and hidden details are
removed from every result.

## Tips

- **Uploading to a form?** Many still refuse WebP. Check that it accepts the format first,
  or keep the JPG.
- **Smaller files** make pages load faster, which is why websites like WebP.
- The image keeps its full size in pixels. For an exact size limit, use "Compress an image to
  a target size".

## Frequently asked questions

### How much smaller is a WebP?

Often noticeably smaller than the JPG with a similar look, though it depends on the photo.

### Does every program open WebP?

All modern browsers do, and recent phones and computers too. Some older programs and many
upload forms do not. When in doubt, keep a JPG copy.

### Will I see a difference in quality?

Hardly. The WebP is saved at a high quality.
