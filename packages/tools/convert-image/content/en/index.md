Photos and images come in different formats, and not every phone, program or website opens all
of them. Choose the format you need, add your photos, and download copies that open
everywhere. It works with HEIC (iPhone), WebP, PNG, AVIF and JPG.

## How it works

1. Choose the format to convert to: **JPG**, **PNG** or **WebP**.
2. Add one or more photos. You can add up to 20 at once.
3. Download each result, or all of them at once as a ZIP file.

The photo keeps its full size in pixels. Nothing is made smaller, and sideways photos come out
the right way up.

## Your photos stay private

Everything happens in your browser. Your photos are never uploaded, so nobody else can see
them. Hidden details stored in photos, such as the place where they were taken and the camera
model, are removed from every result.

## Tips

- **Not sure which format?** Choose **JPG**. It opens on everything and almost every form
  accepts it.
- **Keep transparent areas** (logos, stickers) by choosing **PNG** or **WebP**. JPG fills them
  with white.
- **A photo that is already in the format you chose** keeps its image exactly as it is. Only
  the hidden details are removed.
- Need a smaller file instead? Use "Compress an image to a target size".

## Frequently asked questions

### Will my photo look worse?

Hardly. JPG and WebP files are saved at a high quality that looks like the original, and PNG
loses nothing at all. The file can be bigger or smaller than before depending on the formats.

### Can I convert many photos at once?

Yes, up to 20 at a time, each up to 50 MB. Download them one by one or together as a ZIP file.

### Does it change the size of my photo?

No. The width and height stay the same.
