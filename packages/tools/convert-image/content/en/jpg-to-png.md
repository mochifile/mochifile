Need a PNG instead of a JPG? Add your photos here and download them as PNG, a format that
does not lose more quality each time you edit and save it.

## How it works

1. Check that **PNG** is selected.
2. Add your JPG photos. You can add up to 20 at once.
3. Download each PNG, or all of them as a ZIP file.

## Your photos stay private

Everything happens in your browser. Your photos are never uploaded, and hidden details are
removed from every result.

## Tips

- **PNG files are bigger** than JPG files, often several times bigger. That is normal.
- **Converting does not improve the photo.** The PNG looks exactly like the JPG; it just
  does not get worse when you save it again.
- **Need a transparent background?** Converting does not remove the background. Use an editor
  for that, then save as PNG.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why is my PNG so much bigger?

PNG saves every pixel exactly, while JPG saves a lighter approximation. Photos are the
worst case for PNG.

### Will the PNG look better than the JPG?

It looks the same. The detail lost when the JPG was first saved cannot come back.

### When is PNG the right choice?

For screenshots, logos, drawings with sharp edges and images you will edit more than once.
