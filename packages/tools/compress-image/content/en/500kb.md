500 KB is a comfortable size for sharing photos: email attachments, online listings,
marketplaces and portals that refuse the large files phones produce. This page starts at
500 KB: add your photos and download copies of 500 KB or less.

## How the photo gets to 500 KB

The quality is lowered first. If that is not enough, or if the photo is over 16 megapixels,
it is also made smaller in pixels, and the result shows its new dimensions.

## Your photos stay private

Your photos are processed in your browser and never uploaded. Before you share them, hidden
details such as the place where they were taken are removed.

## Tips for sharing

- **Selling something online?** A smaller file uploads faster, especially on a weak
  connection.
- **Emailing many photos?** Compress them all, then attach the ZIP file, so the email stays
  under your provider's limit.
- **WebP** is accepted by fewer sites than JPG. When in doubt, keep "Same as original" or
  choose JPG.
