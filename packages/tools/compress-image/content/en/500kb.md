500 KB is a comfortable size for sharing photos: email attachments, online listings,
marketplaces and portals that refuse the large files phones produce. Photos stay sharp at
this size. This page starts at 500 KB: add your photos and download copies of 500 KB or less.

## How it works

1. Check that **500 KB** is selected.
2. Add your photos, up to 20 at a time.
3. Download them one by one or all together as a ZIP file.

Most photos reach 500 KB with only a small change in quality and no change in pixel size.

## Your photos stay private

Your photos are processed in your browser and never uploaded. Before you share them, hidden
details such as the place where they were taken are removed.

## Tips for sharing

- **Selling something online?** Photos at 500 KB still show details buyers look for, and
  they upload quickly even on a weak connection.
- **Emailing many photos?** Compress them all, then attach the ZIP file, so the email stays
  under your provider's limit.
- **WebP** makes even smaller files, but some sites do not accept it. When in doubt, keep
  "Same as original" or choose JPG.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why remove the location from photos I share?

Photos taken with phones often record exactly where they were taken, for example your home.
Removing it before sharing keeps that private.

### My photo was already under 500 KB. Did anything change?

Only the hidden details were removed. The image itself is exactly the same.

### Is there a limit on how many photos I can compress?

Up to 20 at a time, each up to 50 MB. When they are done, clear the list and add more.
