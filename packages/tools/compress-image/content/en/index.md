Many websites refuse photos above a size limit: "maximum 100 KB", "file must be under 50 KB".
Pick the limit you need, add your photos, and download copies that are guaranteed to be at or
under it. The photo keeps as much quality as the limit allows.

## How it works

1. Choose the maximum size, or type your own.
2. Choose how to save it. "Same as original" keeps JPG as JPG and PNG as PNG whenever it can.
3. Add one or more photos (JPG, PNG or WebP).
4. Download each result, or all of them at once as a ZIP file.

First the quality is lowered, a little at a time, until the photo fits. The photo is made
smaller only if lowering the quality is not enough, and never larger.

## Your photos stay private

Everything happens in your browser. Your photos are never uploaded, so nobody else can see
them. Hidden details stored in photos, such as the place where they were taken and the
camera model, are removed from every result.

## Tips

- Check the limit on the form you are filling in, and pick that exact size.
- If a form only accepts JPG, choose **JPG** under "Save as".
- A photo with a plain background reaches small sizes with better quality.
- Cropping the photo first (to just your face, or just the document) leaves more detail for
  what matters.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is 50 KB the same as 50,000 bytes?

Some sites count 1 KB as 1,000 bytes and others as 1,024 bytes. Results are always at or
under the smaller number (50,000 bytes for 50 KB), so they pass both.

### Will my photo look worse?

Only as much as needed. If your photo already fits, its image is not changed at all. Small
limits, like 20 KB, do make photos visibly softer.

### Can I compress several photos at once?

Yes, up to 20 at a time. You can download them one by one or together as a ZIP file.
