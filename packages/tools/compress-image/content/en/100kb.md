  - question: "Why is my result smaller than 100 KB?"
    answer: >-
      The search for the best quality stops after a few tries, or once a result uses about 97%
      of the limit, and a photo that fits at the highest quality we use is not made any
      bigger. Either way, it is always at or under 100 KB.
  - question: "Why does my result have fewer pixels?"
    answer: >-
      When the lowest quality we use is still too big for 100 KB, the photo is also made
      smaller in pixels. Photos over 16 megapixels are made smaller too, so they fit in your
      device's memory. The result shows the old and new dimensions.
  - question: "Are my photos sent over the internet?"
    answer: >-
      No. The page downloads its own code, and the compressing happens on your device, in your
      browser. Your photos are never sent anywhere.

100 KB is a common limit for online applications, school and course registrations, and
profile pictures on official sites. This page starts at 100 KB: add your photo and download a
copy that is 100 KB or less.

## How the photo gets to 100 KB

The quality is lowered first, and only as much as needed. If the lowest quality we use is
still too big, the photo is also made smaller in pixels, and the result tells you.

## Your photos stay private

The work happens on your device, in your browser. Nothing is uploaded. Location, camera and
other hidden details are removed from the results.

## Tips for 100 KB

- **Profile and application photos** look best with your face well lit from the front and a
  simple background.
- **Several photos for one form?** Add them all at once, then use "Download all (ZIP)".
- **PNG screenshots** may be saved as JPG to reach 100 KB; the result tells you when that
  happens. Choose WebP if you need to keep transparent areas.
