100 KB is a common limit for online applications, school and course registrations, and
profile pictures on official sites. It leaves enough room for a sharp face photo. This page
starts at 100 KB: add your photo and download a copy that is 100 KB or less.

## How it works

1. Check that **100 KB** is selected.
2. Add one photo or several.
3. Download each result, or all of them as a ZIP file.

At 100 KB most photos keep their full size in pixels: only the quality is lowered, and only
as much as needed.

## Your photos stay private

The work happens on your device, in your browser. Nothing is uploaded. Location, camera and
other hidden details are removed from the results.

## Tips for 100 KB

- **Profile and application photos** look best with your face well lit from the front and a
  simple background.
- **Several photos for one form?** Add them all at once, then use "Download all (ZIP)".
- **PNG screenshots** may be saved as JPG to reach 100 KB; the result tells you when that
  happens. Choose WebP if you need to keep transparent areas.

## Frequently asked questions

### Will the photo still be sharp?

Usually, yes. 100 KB is enough for a clear photo at screen size. Very detailed photos may
lose a little fine texture.

### Why is my result smaller than 100 KB?

The tool stops when the result is close to the limit and still under it. A result of 96 or
97 KB uses almost all the room and always passes.

### Are my photos sent over the internet?

No. The page needs a connection only to load; compressing happens entirely on your device,
and your photos are never sent anywhere.
