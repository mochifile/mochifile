20 KB is one of the strictest limits you will meet online. It shows up in fields for a scanned
signature, a thumbnail or a small ID-style photo. This page starts at 20 KB: add your photo
and download a copy that is 20 KB or less.

## How the photo gets to 20 KB

To reach 20 KB, the quality is lowered first. If that is not enough, the photo is also made
smaller in pixels, which is usually needed at this size. The result tells you its new size,
and its new dimensions when they changed.

## Your photos stay private

Your photo is processed in your browser and never uploaded. Hidden details such as location
and camera model are removed.

## Tips for 20 KB

- **Crop first.** A photo of just your face, or just the signature, keeps far more detail
  than a whole scene squeezed into 20 KB.
- **Signatures:** sign with a dark pen on plain white paper and take the photo in good light,
  without shadows. Clean contrast compresses better.
- **Plain backgrounds help.** A wall or sheet of paper behind you costs almost nothing.
- If the form says "JPG only", choose **JPG** under "Save as".
