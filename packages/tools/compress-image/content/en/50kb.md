"Photo must not exceed 50 KB" is a common rule on government services, exam registrations and
job application portals. This page starts at 50 KB: add your photo and download a copy that is
50 KB or less, with as much quality as the limit allows.

## How it works

1. Check that **50 KB** is selected.
2. Add your photo. You can add several at once.
3. Download the result: it says how big it is now, so you can compare with the limit.

The quality is lowered first. The photo is made smaller in pixels only if that is not enough.
A typical phone photo reaches 50 KB with a clear, recognisable result.

## Your photos stay private

Your photos never leave your device; nothing is uploaded. Hidden details such as the location
where the photo was taken are removed, which matters for documents you send to strangers.

## Tips for forms

- **Read the whole requirement.** Forms often ask for a format too ("JPG or JPEG"). Choose
  **JPG** under "Save as" if so.
- **Face photos:** use a plain, light background, face the camera and crop to your head and
  shoulders before compressing.
- **Documents:** photograph them flat, in daylight, filling the frame. Text stays readable
  at 50 KB when the page fills the picture.
- **Upload refused anyway?** Some sites check the size in a slightly different way. Try the
  next smaller size, like 40 KB, using "Other size".

## Frequently asked questions

### My result is 49 KB. Will a "50 KB" form accept it?

Yes. Results are always at or under 50,000 bytes, which is below 50 KB whether a site counts
1 KB as 1,000 or 1,024 bytes.

### My photo was already under 50 KB. What changed?

Nothing in the image. Only hidden details, such as location, were removed, so you can safely
send it.

### Can I use this on my phone?

Yes. It works in the browser on phones, tablets and computers, without installing anything.
