Many portals, booking sites and email services accept photos only up to 1 MB, while phones
often take photos of 3 to 10 MB. At 1 MB a photo keeps almost all of its quality. This page
starts at 1 MB: add your photos and download copies of 1 MB or less.

## How it works

1. Check that **1 MB** is selected.
2. Add your photos.
3. Download the results.

Most photos reach 1 MB with no visible difference. Only very large photos, such as those from
high-resolution cameras, are also reduced in pixels.

## Your photos stay private

Nothing is uploaded: the work happens in your browser. Hidden details such as location and
camera model are removed from the results.

## Tips for 1 MB

- **Want to print the photo later?** Keep the original too. 1 MB is great for screens and
  uploads, but printing large needs the full-size file.
- **Large PNG images** that cannot fit as PNG are saved as JPG, and the result tells you. If
  the image has transparent areas, choose WebP to keep them.
- **Need even smaller?** Pick 500 KB or type another size under "Other size".

## Frequently asked questions

### Is 1 MB the same as 1,000 KB?

Here, yes: 1 MB is 1,000,000 bytes. Sites that count 1 MB as 1,048,576 bytes accept the
result too, since it is always smaller.

### Will I notice a difference?

Usually not on a phone or computer screen. Only when zooming in a lot on very detailed photos.

### Can I compress photos from a professional camera?

Yes, up to 50 MB per file. Very large photos are reduced in pixels to fit in memory and in
the limit.
