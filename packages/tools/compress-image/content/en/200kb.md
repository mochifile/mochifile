200 KB is a typical limit for uploading documents: a photo of an ID card, a receipt, a proof
of address or a certificate. It is also a good size for photos on websites and forums. This
page starts at 200 KB: add your images and download copies that are 200 KB or less.

## How it works

1. Check that **200 KB** is selected.
2. Add your photos or scans.
3. Download the results.

At 200 KB, most phone photos keep their full size and good quality. Only very large photos
are reduced in pixels.

## Your photos stay private

Documents often contain personal data, so this matters: your files are processed in your
browser and never uploaded. Hidden details such as location are removed too.

## Tips for documents

- **Photograph documents flat** and from directly above, in daylight, so the text is sharp.
- **Fill the frame with the page.** Less background means more detail for the text.
- **Check that the text is readable** in the result before you send it.
- **A scan saved as PNG** can stay PNG if it fits; otherwise it is saved as JPG, and the
  result tells you.

## Frequently asked questions

### Will text in my document stay readable?

At 200 KB, almost always. If small print is hard to read, crop the photo to the page and
compress again.

### Can I send the same document to several sites?

Yes. Once downloaded, the result is a normal image file you can upload anywhere.

### Does this change the colours of my photo?

They look the same. Photos from some recent phones use extra-wide colours, which are converted
to the standard colours every screen and website understands; the difference is hard to see.
