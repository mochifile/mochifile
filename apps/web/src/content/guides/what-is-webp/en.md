WebP is an image format made for the web. It usually gives a smaller file than JPG or PNG at similar quality, which is why many websites now serve their pictures as WebP, and why a photo you save from a page may end up as a .webp file.

## How to open a WebP file

Current browsers open WebP directly: drag the file into a browser window. Most recent photo viewers and phones open it too. Older programs and some upload forms may not, and that is when a conversion helps.

## How to turn a WebP into a JPG

1. Open [Compress image](/compress-image/).
2. Add the WebP file.
3. Under **Save as**, choose JPG.
4. Download the result.

## JPG, PNG or WebP?

- **JPG** works almost everywhere and suits photos.
- **PNG** keeps every pixel exactly and suits screenshots, logos and anything with sharp edges or transparency.
- **WebP** is the smallest of the three for most images, but not every program accepts it yet.

> Your file is converted inside your browser and is not uploaded.
