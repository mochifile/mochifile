Photos from a phone often carry hidden details called EXIF data. They can include the date and time, the phone model and, if location is on, the GPS coordinates of where you stood.

## What can be hidden in a photo

- The exact place where the photo was taken.
- The date and time.
- The camera or phone model.
- Sometimes a thumbnail and text notes.

Anyone who gets the original file can read these details. Some websites remove them when you post, but you cannot count on every site to do it.

## How to remove them with Mochifile

1. Open [Compress image](/compress-image/) and add your photo.
2. Download the result.

Results are rebuilt from the pixels, so they carry no EXIF, GPS, XMP or text metadata. When a photo is already under the size limit, Mochifile removes the metadata without recompressing it.

> All of this happens in your browser. The photo is not uploaded.

## Other things to check

- Turn off location for the Camera app in your phone's settings if you never want it saved.
- What is visible in the photo can also give a place away: street signs, house numbers, a view from a window.
