Many job, school and government portals reject photos over 50 KB. A phone photo is usually 2 to 5 MB, so it has to shrink about 60 times. Here is how to do it without ending up with a blurry, blocky picture.

## Why 50 KB?

Portals set small limits to keep their storage and their pages light. The limit is about the file size, not about how the photo looks on screen, so the goal is the smallest file that still looks right at the size the portal shows it.

## The fastest way

1. Open [Compress image](/compress-image/) and choose **50 KB**.
2. Add your photo. JPG, PNG and WebP work, up to 20 at a time.
3. Download the result. It is checked against the limit before you save it.

> The photo never leaves your phone or computer. It is compressed inside your browser, and hidden details such as your location are removed.

## If the photo still will not fit

Very detailed photos, like landscapes or crowds, sometimes cannot reach 50 KB at full size. Mochifile then makes the photo smaller in pixels and tells you the new size. For an ID photo that is usually fine: portals rarely show more than a few hundred pixels.

## Check before you upload

- Is it the right format? Most portals ask for JPG.
- Does the portal also ask for exact pixel dimensions?
- Is the face, document or text still easy to read?

## Common questions

### Is 50 KB the same as 50,000 bytes?

Some sites count 1 KB as 1,000 bytes and others as 1,024. Results are always at or under 50,000 bytes, so they pass both.

### Will the photo look blurry?

Some fine detail is lost, because that is how a file gets 60 times smaller. Zoom in on the result before you upload: if the face or the text is still clear, it is good enough for the portal.
