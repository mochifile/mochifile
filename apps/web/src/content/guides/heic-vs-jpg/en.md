Recent iPhones save photos as HEIC by default. HEIC files are often much smaller than JPG at similar quality, but fewer programs and websites understand them.

## What is the difference?

- **HEIC** saves space on your phone. Windows, many Android phones, and most upload forms may not open it without extra help.
- **JPG** is bigger, but nearly every device, program and website accepts it.

## When to use which

Keep HEIC while the photos stay on your iPhone and in your Apple apps. Use JPG when you share a photo with someone on another kind of device, or upload it to a form.

## Make your iPhone save JPG

On an iPhone, open Settings, then Camera, then Formats, and choose **Most Compatible**. New photos are then saved as JPG. Photos you already took stay as they are.

## A note about Mochifile

Compress image accepts JPG, PNG and WebP. It does not open HEIC files yet, so convert HEIC photos to JPG first, or use the iPhone setting above for new ones.

> Compressing a JPG in Mochifile happens in your browser. The photo is not uploaded.
