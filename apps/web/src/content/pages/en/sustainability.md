Mochifile exists to make files smaller. Efficiency is not a marketing add-on for us; it is the product. Here is an honest account of what that means for our footprint, and what it does not.

## Smaller files, less to move

Every photo you shrink takes less energy to send across the network and less to load on someone’s phone. A lighter page moves fewer bytes on every visit, for as long as it stays online. The most sustainable byte is the one nobody has to send.

## Your device does the work

Browser tools run on the phone or computer you are already using. There is no upload, no server waiting for your file and no copy stored anywhere, so a typical job adds only a short burst of work on your own device. The heavy code is downloaded once and then cached.

## Light pages, no servers to keep running

The website is a set of static pages served from Cloudflare’s network, with no JavaScript on pages that do not need it.

## What we do not claim

> “Mochifile is not carbon neutral, and we have not bought offsets for our own footprint. What we can stand behind is simple: a product that reduces data transfer, tools that run on hardware you already own, and a site that wastes very little. If we ever measure or offset our emissions, we will say so here, with evidence.”
