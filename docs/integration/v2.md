## Added on top (this branch only)

1. **Navigation**: the header, phone menu and footer link the pages that exist (Solutions, Guides,
   Docs, Pricing, Sign in, Get started, About, Contact, Sustainability, Architecture, API docs, View
   all solutions, Read guides), mark the current page and its section (`aria-current`), and keep
   "Coming soon" for what has no page: the four languages without pages, the Chrome extension, the
   CLI, HEIC/WebP/PDF/video tools, Ask AI, Sponsor.
2. **Legal pages stay unlinked.** #73's pages exist at their own URLs and say they are drafts. The
   footer's "Terms of Service", "Privacy Policy", "Terms & Conditions" and "Data Processing
   Agreement" remain "Coming soon" so nothing presents them as policies in force. Link them only
   when a lawyer has written the text and the drafts' labels are removed.
3. **Cross-links**: Home → Ask and Architecture, Pricing → Architecture, Ask → Pricing.
4. **V2 visual pass on the pages built with design system v1** (#64, #66, #68, #70), using the
   shell's tokens and without changing behaviour: `text-accent` for eyebrows and links, hairline
   `card-line` borders with `shadow-card` / `shadow-chip` on cards, chips and search, the 1,040 px
   inner width (`max-w-site-inner`) on Guides and Docs and 720 px (`max-w-narrow`) on About,
   Sustainability and Architecture, `rounded-card` / `rounded-mascot`, and the V2 type styles
   (`type-page-title`, `type-section-title`, `type-block-title`, `type-eyebrow`, `type-pill`
   at 12 px, `type-prose`). Form inputs keep their control border (contrast).

## Review fixes (independent review of #74)

1. **Header layout shift**: the real links of Solutions, Guides, Docs and Pricing had lost
   `data-shell-link`, the hook of the fixed cell widths from #67, so on Linux the Portuguese "Docs"
   cell moved by 1 px. The links (desktop and phone menu) have it again; a spec guards it.
2. **CI budget**: the suite is ~430 tests per browser and WebKit stopped at Playwright's 12 minute
   budget (358 run, 67 not run). `ci.yml` now runs each browser in three shards (`E2E shard
   (browser n/3)`), every test still runs in every browser, and a small `E2E (browser)` job per
   browser keeps the names the repository ruleset requires.
3. **Form fields** on Contact, Sign in and Sign up have the control border again (3.4:1 or more on
   their ground; checked in `integration-v2.spec.ts`), and so does the Google button.
4. **Pricing** no longer says "$0 forever" (now "$0 for browser tools") nor that "server tools delete
   them when the job ends".
5. **Legal pages (#73)** use the V2 scale (60/40 px H1, accent eyebrow, hairline cards, 720 px) and
   keep their draft labels.
6. **Guide articles**: H1 at 60 px, 40 px on a phone, as in the artboard.
7. **About, Architecture and Solutions** no longer describe how servers will treat files (encrypted
   connection, memory, deletion): no tool needs a server today, and the pages say so.

## #76, the 404 pages

- The V2 `NotFoundPage.astro` replaces the old one (the integration's earlier `pt-3` tweak to the
  old page went with it). Answers stay real 404s with `noindex`, no canonical or alternates, the
  CSP and `X-Frame-Options: DENY` from `_headers`, and the right `lang` per section.
- "See all tools" goes to `/solutions/` and `/pt/solucoes/` (Solutions exists here); the page
  falls back to a tool or the home page only on a build without Solutions.
- V2 tokens: `type-page-title` (60 px, 40 px on a phone), `type-eyebrow` in `text-accent`,
  `type-button-lg` pills at `target-lg` (52 px, as the artboard; the spec's minimum was lowered
  from 56 to 52), `shadow-action` / `shadow-card` / `border-card-line`, `rounded-mascot`.
- Light only: like the other V2 pages, a full-width `.nf` wrapper repeats the light token values
  (`styles/not-found.css`), so a dark system theme does not change it.
- The seven core messages `not_found_*` (en and pt) had no consumers once the page was replaced
  (the page uses `notfound_*` in `messages/not-found/`), and were removed. The old
  `mascot-error.svg` / `mascot-idle.svg` copies in `apps/web/src/assets/mascot` are unused too;
  they mirror the design-system files and were left alone.

## #75 and #77, the compressor

Merged in the required order and by commit, #75 then #77 (which is stacked on it): the history
keeps their ancestry (`git merge-base --is-ancestor` holds) and no commit is duplicated. The two
original branches were not touched.

- Kept as built: the local processing in the worker, the ZIP, limits, cancel and resume, results
  and downloads, the 22 states, and the flow *select files → set options → Compress*, with their
  unit and E2E tests (adding files while compressing, cancelling, resuming, ZIP, downloads on a
  phone).
- `component-constants.css`, the lockfile and the tool layout merged without conflict: the
  compressor only adds panel values, and the old layout of #55, #58, #59, #60 and #63 is not
  merged, so it is not recovered.
- Conflicts: `site-header.spec.ts` (the menu's pointer test: the integration's blank-gap press
  kept, plus #75's check that the "20 KB" option was not chosen), `settings.json` (one more
  message folder, `tool`), and `home-page.spec.ts` (the Home V2 spec kept; its "what works leads
  to real pages" test now reads the 50 KB page's H1 as the browser does, because #77 marks up
  the title's last words).
- Breadcrumb: `Home › Solutions › tool [› variant]`, as in the artboard, with Solutions at
  `/solutions/` and `/pt/solucoes/` (also in the page's JSON-LD). `tool-variant.spec.ts` follows.
- Light only: `styles/tool-page-light.css` repeats the light value of every colour token on the
  tool page wrapper (a unit test checks it against `tokens.json`) and gives it the page ground at
  full width, so the page and its panel stay light under a dark system theme. The page and panel
  styles already used the V2 tokens (`accent`, `card-line`, shadows) through their fallbacks, which
  now resolve to the real tokens.
- `tool-reserve` was not recalibrated: the layout-shift, fold and reserve specs pass unchanged.
