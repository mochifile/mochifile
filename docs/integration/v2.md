## Final audit fixes (P1)

1. **Light theme at the root.** `BaseLayout.astro` now renders `<html data-theme="light">` and
   `<meta name="color-scheme" content="light">`. The tokens' dark theme only applies when
   `data-theme` is not `light` (or is forced to `dark`), so no page follows the visitor's
   `prefers-color-scheme: dark` any more; the header wordmark is always the light file. The
   existing mechanism is kept (the token generator, its test, the forced-theme hooks) and the V2
   pages' own light wrappers stay. `integration-v2.spec.ts` loads every page of the sitemap and the
   404s of both languages, at 1440 and 390 px, with the system set to dark, and checks `data-theme`,
   `color-scheme`, the page and text colours; the phone menu is checked too. The wordmark spec no
   longer expects a dark wordmark for a dark system.
2. **Compressor result chips on a phone (state 11).** `.cp-tag` was `white-space: nowrap`, so
   "Transparency filled with white" ran under the Download button below ~390 px. The chip now wraps
   inside its column (`max-width: 100%`, `overflow-wrap: anywhere`, two-line height) and `.cp-facts`
   can shrink, so the button keeps its place and size. `compress-image-rows.spec.ts` compresses a
   transparent PNG with a long file name at 320, 360, 375 and 390 px in English and Portuguese and
   compares the boxes: no tile, name, size, chip or note overlaps the button or leaves the row, the
   button stays 44 px and at the row's edge, and neither the row nor the page overflows. It failed
   before the fix. The other 21 states and the processing are untouched.

## Content fix before publication: Sign in and Sign up

Accounts and e-mail sign-in do not exist, so the two pages no longer promise what they would give.
Layouts, behaviour (the forms only open their "not available yet" message) and the V2 look are
unchanged; English and Portuguese were edited together.

- **Said first:** a "Preview" note at the top of the card, before the Google button and the e-mail
  field: accounts and sign-in by e-mail are not available yet, the page shows how they are planned
  to look, and nothing is sent when a button is pressed (`role="note"`, ahead of the form in the
  reading order).
- **Benefits list** (now "What to know about accounts"): "Higher limits: more files, and bigger
  ones, than without an account" is gone, because the limits of the browser tools do not depend on
  an account; it is replaced by "Browser tools need no account: they work without signing up, and
  their limits do not depend on an account". "The free plan needs no credit card" became "The free
  browser tools need no sign-up and no credit card".
- **Sign-up subtitle:** "Accounts are not available yet. The free browser tools need no account."
- **No present-tense promise of a code:** the note under the button says sign-in is *planned* to use
  a one-time code when accounts open; the preview screens say "is how the next step will look" and
  "codes would only work for a short time".
- Left as is: "By creating an account you agree to the Terms of Service and the Privacy Policy" (plain
  text, no link, and no account can be created; the legal pages are drafts) and the plans on the
  Pricing page.
- `integration-v2.spec.ts` checks, in both languages and on both pages, that the note is visible
  and above the e-mail field, that none of the old promises is on the page, that the form sends no
  request and only opens its message; `auth-pages.spec.ts` follows the new wording.

## Final adjustments after the preview review

1. **FAQ closed by default.** `open={index === 0}` was on the Home, Solutions and tool-page FAQs
   (Pricing already started closed); it is gone, so every question starts collapsed on every page
   and opens on click, Enter or Space (native `<details>`, nothing else changed). The Home,
   Solutions and tool-page specs now expect none open, and `integration-v2.spec.ts` loads ten
   pages (Home, Pricing, Solutions, tool pages, both languages) and checks that none starts open
   at 1440 and 390 px and that the first one opens with Enter and closes with Space.
2. **Language selector in the footer only.** The header's language button and list, and the language
   chips in the phone menu, are removed (with their width cells in `component-constants.css`, the
   popover positioning in `global.css`, the unused `--spacing-language-menu` and the unused message
   `nav_language_current`). The footer keeps its six options in their own names: English and
   Português (Brasil) are links, the other four are "Coming soon" buttons; no translation was added.
   The header row is the artboard's: the logo at the left edge of the 1,104 px container and Get
   started at the right edge, with nothing empty in between; the phone menu is the four links, the
   two buttons and the mascot. `site-header.spec.ts`, `home.spec.ts` and `language-suggestion.spec.ts`
   use the footer, and a spec checks the header and menu have no language control at 320-1440 px.
3. **Sharper category card images (Home, Ask, guide aside).** They were q80 copies of the
   artwork: `assets/home/use-*.webp` (354-366 px, ~7 KB, no `srcset`, so a Retina screen enlarged
   them 1.7x) and `assets/ask/use-*` (enlarged to 640 px from the same 354 px). The approved source
   is `docs/design-ref/v2/assets/use-*.png` (354-366 x 216 px), and **no larger original exists**
   (checked: the repository, the design export, `~/Desktop/v2`, the older export in Downloads), so
   the illustrations were not redrawn or re-composed. Each now has two files made from the original
   PNG (`assets/cards/`, `lib/card-images.ts`): a near-lossless copy of the original pixels (~35 KB)
   for 1x screens and a Lanczos 2x enlargement with a light unsharp mask (~20 KB) for 2x screens,
   chosen by `srcset` with `sizes`; both carry width and height, so the box is reserved and nothing
   shifts. At DPR 2 a 1440 px desktop and a 390 px phone get the 2x file (the 592 and 628 px the
   boxes need are covered by its 708-732 px). The 641-1,100 px range, where the cards are wider
   (up to ~560 css px), is still limited by the 354 px source. Evidence: `docs/integration/images/
   cards-before-after.webp` (DPR 2 captures, mascot detail, before left, after right). A true 2x
   would need the original illustrations at 700 px or more.
